Seria Tematyczna

Interactive Storytelling

An immersive English series of 8 guided sessions linked by a common story, where YOU decide what will happen. A Role-Play Game (RPG) designed specifically to encourage speaking and listening, to build and recall vocabulary, to think creatively, apply knowledge and work on communication together.

  • Adventurous scenarios and thrilling stories
  • Work together and communicate your ideas
  • Builds vocabulary week by week
  • Make decisions and lead your team
  • Earn badges and keep a session journal

90 Min sessions 1 / week
